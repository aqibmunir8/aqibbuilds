How it works

Three habits. Zero lost friendships.

01

Add the people who matter

Investors, clients, old friends, mentors. If a name is worth remembering, it gets a card — name, context, and nothing else to fill in.

02

Log every touch in seconds

Brunch, email, hallway chat. One line and a date is enough — Porter lines it all up into a timeline per person, automatically.

03

Get nudged before anyone goes cold

Anyone quiet for three weeks surfaces in “Going quiet.” A small reminder is all it takes to keep a relationship warm.
